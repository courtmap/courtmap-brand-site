Brand

A map to your people.

Every city is full of courts, and full of people who'd love a game on them. CourtMap puts both on one map: the courts near you, the games happening on them, and players at your level. The map gets you to the court. Your crew is why you keep coming back.

Help adults find real belonging by making it easy to play with someone new.

Sport is the vehicle and belonging is the destination. CourtMap finds the game, the court and people at your level, then makes it easy to keep playing with the ones you click with. The wordmark is set in Figtree with a capital C and M, as plain and confident as a place name on a map. The mark is two strokes that meet as one ball: two people, one rally.

The brand in three words

Every product, design and partnership decision gets checked against these.

Belonging

The reason we exist. Nobody should move to a new city and wonder where their people are.

Community

How CourtMap feels. Every game is a chance to meet someone, and every next game is a chance to see them again.

Trust

What makes it work. Meeting a stranger on court only happens when you trust who's on the other side of the net.

Trust

Trust is the foundation of the product, so the brand shows it plainly and early. These are the signals players see before they agree to meet someone. The full safety approach lives in the strategy doc.

Logo

Two strokes, one rally.

Each stroke is a person. Neither one closes on its own, but together they make a ball: the thing two strangers share the moment they start to rally. The strokes are the same shape, turned to face each other, so neither one leads. It's a partnership, not a ranking.

Construction

The mark fits a circle 1.65 times the wordmark's ascender height, and the two share a vertical centre. Stroke B is Stroke A rotated 180°: the same shape and the same weight, facing each other as equals.

Clear space

Keep a margin of x around the lockup, where x is the height of the mark. Nothing enters it: no type, no edges, no other logos.

Minimum size

Below these sizes the counters in the wordmark and the inner curves of the mark fill in. Under 96px wide, use the mark on its own.

Colour versions

Use Colour in the product and anywhere the wordmark needs the most contrast, and Brand for marketing on neutral grounds. Use Neutral for one-colour print, embossing and co-branding, and On brand on CourtMap Blue and the brand gradient.

Colour

One confident blue.

Blue is the colour of trust, and it sets us apart from racquet apps that default to greens and blacks. CourtMap Blue is the only brand hue, used brightly and with confidence on the one action that matters. The product is dark-first like evening courts, and Optic is the ball, so it stays small.

Voice

Talk like a friend who knows your name.

Warm, direct and occasionally witty. We never lecture and never oversell. We celebrate the small human moments that make sport social: the rally that went 30 shots, the coffee after, the first time someone texts "same time next week?"

Lead with people

Names before features. "Priya accepted your match" beats "Request accepted."

Celebrate small wins

A first match deserves a moment. A tenth match deserves a milestone. So does a first vouch.

Never intimidate

A first-timer and a club regular should both feel CourtMap is for them.

Be exact

Times, places and costs are the message. Lead with them, keep it short.

Imagery

Real games, real light.

Candid over posed: real people mid-game or mid-laugh. The moment after the match matters as much as the match itself. Two people on a courtside bench, water bottles out, talking. That moment is the product.

Mid-moment

Catch the reach, the laugh or the handshake. Nobody looks at the lens.

After the match

The bench, the coffee, the walk to the car. Shoot the friendship, not just the forehand.

Diverse by default

Toronto is one of the most multicultural cities in the world. Real ages, bodies and backgrounds, in plain kit with no sponsors.

Warm light, blue courts

Golden hour where we can get it, and blue court surfaces that carry the brand colour so the logo doesn't have to.

Illustration

Flat court geometry, one optic ball and a soft ground shadow. Use these for empty and error states in the product. Each comes in a light and a dark version, and this page shows whichever matches your theme.

Motion

Quick off the line.

Motion explains a change; if it only decorates, we cut it. The logo animation is our one flourish: the two strokes swing in from opposite sides and meet: two people, one rally.

Social

@courtmap

Instagram and TikTok are where the crew lives. Lead with the human moment, not the feature. The mix is human stories (40%), community moments (25%), helpful and witty posts (20%) and product (15%), and every post uses one of these templates so the grid reads as one brand.

Headers

LinkedIn speaks to clubs and partners, and X carries launch news. Keep the logo out of the platform's avatar crop zone at bottom left.

The @courtmap handle is a placeholder. Check that it's available on Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and X before publishing.

Merch

Kit you'd actually play in.

Good blanks and small marks. Every piece should work on court first and as a logo second: a left-chest print on tees, the mark alone on caps, and the full lockup only where there's room.
